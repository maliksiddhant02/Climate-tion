# Draki

Field-level flood warnings for cane growers on the Ba River floodplain, Fiji. Draki lays the week's rain forecast over the shape of a farm, shows which part goes under, and texts the farmer three actions plus one line on *why* storms are getting heavier.

*Draki* is Fijian for "weather". Native-speaker check pending.

**Climate Hack-tion 2026 · Track:** Climate Awareness & Education (2035 target: climate action education for all), through climate-resilient farming.

## Run it

```bash
cd web
npm install
npm run dev                    # http://localhost:5173  (open #/live?replay for the Cyclone Cody replay)
node src/lib/flood.check.ts    # flood model self-check
```

## How it works

1. **Field:** a polygon on a satellite map (demo: a 40 ha cane block on the east bank of the Ba River).
2. **Land:** inside the Ba floodplain (`web/public/data/ba/`, built by `scripts/build_ba_data.py`), every 30 m square has its elevation (Copernicus GLO-30), its **height above the Ba River** (HAND) and its land cover (ESA WorldCover). Outside that box the app falls back to rain-only with 90 m elevation.
3. **River:** Ba floods when the Ba River overtops its banks. GloFAS river flow (7-day forecast with 50 ensemble runs live; the 2022 record for the Cyclone Cody replay) is turned into a river level. Any square lower than that level floods.
4. **Rain:** the wettest 72 h in the 7-day forecast, or ERA5 for Cody, pools in the low spots (a "bathtub" fill). Each square takes the deeper of river and rain water.
5. **Calibration:** the river stays in its banks up to its 2-year flood (402 m³/s, from 40 years of GloFAS) and reaches Ba Town's streets at Cody's peak (1,090 m³/s), as Ba Town Council reported.
6. **Validation:** all 4 major recorded Ba River floods since 2009 trigger "Act today"; 5 of 7 recorded floods trigger Act or Watch. Sentinel-1 radar 2.5 days after Cody found no open water left, which is why a forecast, not a satellite map, has to give the warning.
7. **Actions:** a fixed playbook per risk level. Advice is never generated by AI.

## Rebuild the Ba data (optional, no API keys)

```bash
python -m venv scripts/.venv
scripts/.venv/Scripts/pip install -r scripts/requirements.txt     # macOS/Linux: scripts/.venv/bin/pip
scripts/.venv/Scripts/python scripts/build_ba_data.py
```
